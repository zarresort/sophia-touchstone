Our Story

Founded in the heart of our community, North Star Bakery began with a passion for traditional baking and high-quality ingredients. Over the years, we have grown into a warm neighborhood gathering place where fresh artisan breads and pastries are baked daily.

Sourcing Philosophy

We believe in using good, simple ingredients. We get our flour from local farms and take our time baking everything fresh so it tastes great.

Staff Highlights

Our team works hard every day to bring you the best baked goods:

  • Elena (Head Baker): Makes all our sourdough breads.
  • Marcus (Pastry Chef): Bakes our sweet treats and tarts.
